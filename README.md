### About me

Networking and cybersecurity student. I design, configure and verify Cisco networks in Packet Tracer and document every lab the way it would be handed over at work: topology, addressing plan, configs, verification output and troubleshooting notes.

- Working toward **Cisco CCNA 200-301**
- Focused on **network security**: device hardening, secure management, switch security, ACLs
- Interested in both **defensive (SOC / blue team)** and **offensive (pentest / red team)** paths
